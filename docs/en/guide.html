Your first steps

Five steps from installation to your first live show or recording.

  1. Install and launch

    On first launch, RegieWave asks “What do you want to do?”: capture your screen, film yourself with a webcam, edit a video or create a title. Pick what fits, or “I'll explore on my own”.

  2. Add your sources

    In the Live workspace, the toolbox on the left (Media) adds a screen, game or window capture, a webcam, a video, an image or text.

  3. Place them in the preview

    Click an item to select it, drag it to move it, use its handle to resize it. The panel on the right shows its settings.

  4. Set the sound

    The SOUND column, always visible at the bottom right, lists each input (microphone, computer audio, application) with its level. Per-scene sound is possible.

  5. Record or go live

    Record and Go live are at the top, in every workspace. Prepare your destinations in Destinations (or the Streaming tab): stream key, SRT or WHIP address. Switching workspace interrupts nothing.

Find your way

The six workspaces

The tabs at the top of RegieWave pick the workspace. The Tools menu gathers the library, shortcuts and settings.

Direct

Sources, scenes, recording and streaming.

Editing

Video and audio tracks, titles, grading, export.

Composition

Animated titles, 3D, tracking, retouching.

Audio

Mixer, effects, plugins, voice isolation.

Studio

Multiview, transitions, run-of-show, outputs.

Notes

Your production notes and instructions.

The full guide

The detailed guide is inside RegieWave (Help menu): scenes and sources, studio, editing, compositing, grading, sound, export and shortcuts. It is currently in French.

Something wrong?

Help → Create a report prepares a file to attach to a GitHub issue: version, system, graphics card, settings and the end of the logs, without stream keys or passwords.

Alpha version. RegieWave is still in development: many features have only been tried on a single computer, not yet with every webcam, microphone, graphics card and streaming service. Test before an important live show or recording, keep a copy of your projects and report problems : that is what moves the software forward.

About

An all-in-one app, made to last

RegieWave brings together what you usually look for in four apps: live streaming, studio control, editing and compositing.

Free, and yours

No subscription, no watermark. What you create with RegieWave belongs to you, including to monetise it.

Private

RegieWave sends no data to its author. Stream keys stay in the system secure store and are masked in logs.

Simple first

Simple settings by default, everything stays adjustable when you unfold it. It does not try to look like raw OBS.

Safe

Verified updates, a sandbox for web pages (installed Windows version), regular security audits of the libraries.

Terms of use

RegieWave — Direct

Game, screen and window capture, webcam, videos, images, text and web pages.

  • Several destinations at once (Twitch, YouTube, Kick, SRT, WHIP), bitrate that adapts to your connection.
  • Built-in Twitch alerts and chat, subscriber goal, combined Twitch, YouTube and Kick chat.
  • Avatars — PNGtuber, 3D (VRM) and Inochi2D following your voice or face.
  • OBS-friendly: scene import, control through obs-websocket.
RegieWave — Studio

Every scene in view, preview and program side by side.

  • Multiview of every scene, with the on-air scene marked.
  • T-bar, cut and transitions, including video stingers.
  • Run-of-show, lower thirds, teleprompter and a replay memory for slow-motion.
  • Outputs to a screen or a virtual camera, scene by scene.
RegieWave — Editing

Everything works with the mouse, on a multi-track timeline.

  • Video and audio tracks, titles, fades, speed, reverse playback, freeze frames.
  • Colour grading, subtitles, mixing with automation, 5.1 sound.
  • Export H.264, HEVC, AV1, ProRes, plus AAF, OTIO, EDL and FCP XML exchange.
  • Your live recordings open straight in the editor.
RegieWave — Composition

Animated titles and overlays, made once, used everywhere.

  • Layers and keyframes, curves, expressions.
  • 3D: relief, lights, drop shadows, camera.
  • Motion tracking, one-click object cut-out, photo retouching and PSD.
  • A composition becomes a live source or an editing clip.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes: no subscription, no watermark, no hidden paid tier. What you create belongs to you. RegieWave comes without warranty and may not be redistributed anywhere other than this page and its GitHub page.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 (version 1809) or newer, and recent 64-bit Linux (AppImage). There is no Mac version for now.

Can it replace OBS?

RegieWave can import your OBS scenes and be controlled like OBS (obs-websocket). But it is in alpha: keep your current setup for important live shows until you have tried RegieWave with your own hardware.

What about my privacy?

RegieWave sends no data to its author. It only connects to services you choose (Twitch, YouTube…) and, if you ask, to GitHub to look for an update.

Where do I report a problem?

In GitHub issues. In RegieWave, “Help → Create a report” prepares a file to attach, without stream keys or passwords.

Ready to try it?

Free, no subscription, no watermark. Everything is included in the installer.
